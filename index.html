I am a master's student in Electronic Information at Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School, advised by Prof. Xinlei Chen in the AIoT group. I received my bachelor's degree in Communication Engineering from Northeastern University, China, in 2025.

My research focuses on efficient vision-language models and multimodal perception, with an emphasis on visual token pruning and inference optimization. I am also interested in LLM pretraining and long-context modeling, with a broader interest in perception for embodied systems.

Research interests: VLM inference optimization · Multimodal perception · LLM pretraining · Long-context modeling · Long-range dependency data

Publications

EventPrune: Cascaded Event-Assisted Token Pruning for Efficient First-Person Dynamic Spatial Reasoning

Pengtao Ma†, Ziliang Zhou†, Ciyu Ruan, Haoyang Wang, Kaiyuan Li, Zihang Gong, Wenhua Ding, Chen Gao, Jingao Xu, Xinlei Chen

NeurIPS 2026 · Accepted

EventPrune uses event-derived motion cues to prune redundant visual tokens in vision-language models. Its cascaded framework reduces inference cost while retaining the information needed for first-person dynamic spatial reasoning.

[Paper] [arXiv] [NeurIPS]

Event Camera Meets Mobile Embodied Perception: Abstraction, Algorithm, Acceleration, Application

Haoyang Wang, Ruishan Guo, Pengtao Ma, Ciyu Ruan, Xinyu Luo, Wenhua Ding, Tianyang Zhong, Jingao Xu, Yunhao Liu, Xinlei Chen

ACM Computing Surveys, 58(8), Article 206, 2026

This survey organizes event-based mobile perception around four pillars: abstraction, algorithms, acceleration, and applications. It connects event representations and sensor fusion with efficient deployment and tasks such as tracking, odometry, and reconstruction.

[Paper] [ACM] [arXiv]

Low-cost Granary Vehicles Positioning Using a Distributed LED Network

Jingya Liu, Pengtao Ma, Lingmin Zhang, Yu Geng, Lei Yan

IEEE ICCT 2024 · 24th International Conference on Communication Technology, pp. 775–780

A low-cost localization method for granary vehicles using a distributed LED network and inertial navigation. An attitude-aware AAVSD-Kalman filter fuses the two positioning sources to reduce accumulated drift in simulation.
